A friend asked for help finding a life partner for her daughter. She had devoted years to medicine and to caring for a parent. Now she wanted time for a hope of her own.

As I reached out to people I trusted, other families told me much the same story. When I shared the idea with my friend Sandhya, she joined me. Together, we started Divine Match to bring that same care to each introduction we make.

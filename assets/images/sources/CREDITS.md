# Homepage background photography

The bouquet and rings come from the owner-supplied files `bouquet.webp` and `rings.jpeg`. The background embeds the photographs and uses SVG clipping paths to isolate the flowers and bands.

The lit candles are from [Jill Burrow on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/burning-candles-against-white-textile-6069743/), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
